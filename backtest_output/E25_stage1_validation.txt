3  LOOK-AHEAD AUDIT -- does a reading at bar T use a bar after T
==============================================================================

  Method: compute every price-derived reading on frame[:T], then on
  frame[:T+40] TRUNCATED BACK to T. Identical inputs must give
  identical answers; any difference means the longer frame leaked.

  [PASS] readings are a pure function of the bars given (25 names)

  Pivots: levels.swings() claims to return CONFIRMED pivots only.
  A pivot within `depth` bars of the end cannot be confirmed yet, so
  none may be reported there.

  [PASS] no pivot is reported inside the unconfirmable tail (25 names)  -- 0 late pivots

  --------------------------------------------------------------------------
  THE ONE LOOK-AHEAD THAT EXISTS, AND IT IS STRUCTURAL:
  P/E, sector P/E and market cap come from market_snapshot, which
  holds ONE timestamp and is replaced on every pull. For a LIVE scan
  that is correct -- today's figures for today's decision. For any
  historical date it is look-ahead, and the data to fix it does not
  exist in this database.

  CONSEQUENCE: the scanner as built is a LIVE instrument. A historical
  backtest can only use the 75 points that ARE point-in-time --
    MFI 15 + transfer 20 + کد به کد 15 + support 15 + volume 10 = 75
  and must drop valuation (15) and size (10). Rescaled to 100 that is
  a DIFFERENT model with different weights, and it has to be
  preregistered as such rather than described as this scanner.
  --------------------------------------------------------------------------
